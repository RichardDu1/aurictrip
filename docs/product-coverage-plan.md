## 4. Chinese Traveler Content Structure

For each product, Chinese customers expect:

### Page Structure (赞那度 model)
1. **Hero image** — emotional, editorial photography
2. **Title** — poetic Chinese name + duration + destination
3. **Price block** — ¥XX,XXX起/人, with disclaimers
4. **Highlights** — 4-5 unique selling points with icons
5. **Day-by-day itinerary** — detailed, with hotel names embedded
6. **Hotel section** — named hotels with descriptions (CRITICAL for Chinese clients)
7. **Cost breakdown** — included vs excluded (Chinese clients want clarity)
8. **Booking notes** — best season, advance booking requirements
9. **FAQ** — practical concerns (visa, safety, food, children)
10. **Floating CTA** — "与顾问对话" / "定制你的旅程"

### Pricing Psychology (Chinese market)
- Show public prices (not enquiry-only like Black Tomato)
- Label as "指导价" (guide price) with flexibility disclaimer
- Price tiers: ¥30-50K (entry luxury), ¥50-80K (core luxury), ¥80-150K (ultra luxury), ¥150K+ (expedition)
- Chinese clients compare hotels by name — must include hotel brands

### Naming Convention
- Chinese poetic headline: "法老巡礼" (Pharaoh Pilgrimage), "王公之旅" (Royal Journey)
- Descriptive subtitle with key selling points
- NOT like Black Tomato's vague emotional names — Chinese clients want specifics
