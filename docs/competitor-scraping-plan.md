## Active Phase
Identify and catalog all product lines from key competitors. Below is the master checklist.

## English competitors (luxury bespoke operators)

### Black Tomato (blacktomato.com)
**Navigation structure**: By region (14) + By travel type (10+) + By who (9)
**Key regions**: Africa(19 countries), Asia, Europe, Latin America, Middle East, North America, Polar, etc.
**Key types**: Safari, Beach, Adventure, Food & Wine, Culture, Unusual, Eclipse, Field Trip, Get Lost, Blink
**Key "who" segments**: Family, Couples, Groups, Honeymoons, Solo, Multi-generational

### Scott Dunn (scottdunn.com)
**Navigation structure**: Destinations(85+) + Holiday Types + Who travels + Travel Style
**Key regions**: 12 regions covering ~85 countries
**Key types**: Safari, Beach, Ski, Food & Wine, Outdoor & Active, Experiential
**Key who**: Family, Couples, LGBTQ+, Extended Family, Honeymoons
**Key style**: Cruise, Rail, Road Trips, Private Travel (ultra-luxury tier)

## Content to extract per competitor
For each competitor, catalog:
1. All destination/trip listings with names & prices
2. How they structure each product page
3. Price ranges and tiers
4. Unique selling points
5. Content format (day-by-day, hotel names, pricing transparency)
