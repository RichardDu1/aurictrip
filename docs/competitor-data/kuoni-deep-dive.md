## Key Patterns for AuricTrip Product Design:

1. **Public pricing is the norm** — Kuoni shows prices for all hotel packages. Chinese clients expect this.
2. **Named hotels are the #1 selling point** — Every itinerary names specific hotels. Chinese luxury travelers compare by hotel brand.
3. **Multi-centre is the default structure** — Almost all Kuoni trips are 2-3 centre combinations.
4. **Duration sweet spot: 7-14 nights** — 7 nights for single-country, 10-14 for multi-country.
5. **"From £X,XXXpp" pricing** — Always per person, based on 2 sharing, with flights.
6. **Small group tours have set departures** — A&K/Scott Dunn pattern.
7. **Self-drive is popular** — Especially South Africa, Australia, Italy, Spain.
