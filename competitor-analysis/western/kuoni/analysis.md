## 一、品牌定位与核心主张

### 1.1 品牌概述

Kuoni 是英国老牌高端旅行运营商，历史超 100 年（创始人 Alfred Kuoni）。定位为 **"premium tour operator"**（高端旅行运营商），核心卖词:

- "We don't just sell holidays, we make them. The 'making' is in the detail."
- "Pioneers of effortless customisation, because we listen to you and we get you"
- "The original pioneers of holiday magic"

### 1.2 品牌信任三柱 (Kuoni Difference)

| 支柱 | 标题 | 描述要点 |
|------|------|---------|
| 1 | Real expertise, from people who love to travel | 真人旅行专家（非标准脚本），从咨询到归国全程服务 |
| 2 | Support, flexibility & options if plans change | ATOL 保护 + ABTA 会员，FCDO 警告时可全额退款 |
| 3 | Voted best by travellers | Feefo 4.9/5，200+ 奖项，Which?/Times/Condé Nast 认可 |

```
From £X,XXX per person (或 p/p)
X nights | 2 adults
[Board Basis]
[Airline] from [Airport], transfers included
Based on [specific date]
```

### 3.3 四种折扣格式

| 类型 | 格式 | 示例 |
|------|------|------|
| 百分比 | "Save up to XX%" | Save up to 40% |
| 固定金额(双人) | "Save £X,XXX per couple" | Save £4,200 per couple |

| 增值赠送 | "Complimentary Nth night / Free upgrade" | Stay 3 nights, get 4th free |

### 3.4 关键定价策略

- 极低心理门槛：From £1,527 p/p → Maldives 7晚仅 £3,100/两人

- 基础房型+淡季+含机票接送
- 税费透明：当地税费单独标注
