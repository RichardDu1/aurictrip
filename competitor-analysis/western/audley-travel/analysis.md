Your specialist will design your journey from scratch, selecting every element around your passions and preferences. The result is a unique itinerary with a unique price. This itinerary is just an idea, and our guide pricing is an indication of how much this kind of trip might cost, depending on factors like when you travel, how far in advance you plan, where you stay, and what you choose to experience."

## 11. Key Takeaways for Our Project

### Design Patterns Worth Adopting
1. **Destination specialist system**: real travel photos + first-person quotes to build trust
2. **Guide Price transparency**: clear starting prices on every trip card while preserving customization flexibility
3. **Day-by-day expandable itinerary**: Close All / Expand All pattern for information density control
4. **Multi-tier accommodation**: 3-4 options per city across price points
5. **Emotional brand narrative**: "serendipity" framing + transformation-focused testimonials
6. **Dense trust signals**: B Corp, Which?, IATA, AITO, FCDO, plus 98% recommend rate
7. **Trip Finder tool**: faceted search by destination, category, and month
8. **Comprehensive destination FAQ**: accordion covering practical travel info

### Differentiation Opportunities (for Chinese Market)
1. **Language gap**: Audley has zero Chinese-language presence -- blue ocean
2. **Price breakdown**: go beyond guide price to show what drives cost differences
3. **Specialist interaction**: add video intros, live consultation booking, WeChat integration
4. **Cultural content depth**: deeper local content tailored to Chinese travelers' interests
5. **Mobile-first design**: Audley's long-form itinerary pages may be suboptimal on mobile
6. **Payment integration**: WeChat Pay / Alipay for Chinese customers
7. **Social proof**: integrate Xiaohongshu, Douyin content instead of Trustpilot
